METHODOLOGY

How SiteFit scores properties

SiteFit scores each listing with five components. The default weights change with the business type, and you can adjust them in the filter panel.

Business fitProperty type and subtype match
MarketMunicipal population and recent growth
ValueRelative price and your budget
FinancialCap rate when the listing provides it
Data qualityHow much useful listing data is available

Business fit uses explicit rules instead of a black-box model. For example, a restaurant gets a strong fit score for restaurant, QSR, storefront, retail, and similar listing types. An industrial user puts more weight on warehouse, flex, distribution, and industrial space.

Market data currently uses 2024 municipal population estimates and 2020 to 2024 population growth from the North Carolina Office of State Budget and Management. Future versions can add income, traffic, parking, competition, and zoning once those data sources are added.

This is a screening tool. A high score does not verify zoning, environmental conditions, financing, valuation, title, parking, traffic counts, or the economics of a specific business.
