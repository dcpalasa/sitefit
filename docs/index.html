METHODOLOGY

How SiteFit scores properties

SiteFit combines five interpretable signals. The model is intentionally transparent so the score can be explained in an interview, changed in code, and challenged by the user.

Property / business fit35%
Local market momentum25%
Relative value20%
Financial signal10%
Data completeness10%

Market momentum uses municipal population and 2020–2024 population growth from the North Carolina Office of State Budget and Management. Property fit uses an explicit compatibility matrix between the selected business use and listing type/subtype. Value is relative to comparable listings in the current dataset and the user's budget.

This is a screening tool. A high score does not verify zoning, environmental conditions, financing, valuation, title, or the economics of a specific operating business.
